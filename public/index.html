SAFETY CENTER

Recognize the warning signs.

Small habits can prevent serious security incidents.

01

Pause before acting

Urgency is a common manipulation technique. Slow down and verify unexpected requests through an official channel.

02

Inspect the sender

Look for misspelled domains, unusual addresses, and display names that imitate trusted organizations.

03

Protect credentials

Never provide passwords or verification codes through email, text, or an unfamiliar login page.

04

Report early

Reporting a suspicious message—even if you did not click it—can protect other people from the same campaign.
